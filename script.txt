In this town, every time you sneeze, something from your house disappears. And it all lands under the bridge. That's why nobody here sells pepper. I was pretty careful. But my best friend Owen had allergies. His house was down to one chair. Meanwhile, the bridge monster had everything. Forty couches. Six hundred spoons. And my grandma's fridge. Then my nose started itching. Achoo! My bed was gone. Mom yelled, James, where's your bed? And that's when I had an idea. I'd take it back from the bridge. First, I tried sneaking in. He sat on me. So I asked nicely. He just growled. Then he sniffled. So I gave him a tissue. He sneezed, and my bed flew home. So I started bringing him soup, warm socks, and pepper. By lunchtime my room was as full as his bridge. But then I saw Owen, alone in his empty house. I realized I'd spent all day on my stuff and ignored my best friend. So I brought the monster to Owen's. I asked, can you sneeze for my best friend? The monster sniffed the pepper. Achoo! Owen's couch came back. Achoo! His chair too. Owen handed me the pepper. Achoo! My bed went back to the bridge. Owen said, sleep over. But I get the chair. I stared at him. Best sneeze ever.
